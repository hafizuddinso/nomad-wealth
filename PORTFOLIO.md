# Portfolio Description

## Nomad Wealth — Fintech Dashboard for Digital Nomads

I designed and developed Nomad Wealth, a responsive personal finance dashboard for people who manage money across multiple countries, currencies, banks and investment types.

The application includes multi-currency account tracking, income and expense management, simple budgeting, safe-to-spend guidance, emergency-fund calculations, travel planning, investment tracking, and working loan, stock, investment and mutual-fund calculators.

**Role:** Product concept, UX/UI design, responsive front-end development and financial calculator development.

**Technology:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript, localStorage, Service Worker, GitHub and Vercel.
